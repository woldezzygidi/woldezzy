05 Common questions

What people ask first.

How much does SEO cost in the West Midlands?

SEO retainers across the West Midlands typically run from £1,500 to £5,000 per month depending on site size, competition and scope. One-off technical audits usually sit between £2,500 and £8,000.

Every engagement is quoted in scope rather than hours. I do not do day rates for strategic work.

Do you work with businesses across Birmingham, Coventry and the Black Country, not just Wolverhampton?

Yes. I take on clients across the whole region: Birmingham, Wolverhampton, Coventry, Dudley, Walsall, Solihull, Sandwell, Stoke-on-Trent, Telford and the surrounding areas. Most work is remote with in-person meetings where they add value.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Local SEO for a well-scoped business usually shows measurable movement within 8 to 12 weeks. Broader organic growth for competitive regional terms typically takes 6 to 12 months.

Anyone promising faster is either overstating or doing something that will not last.

What kinds of businesses do you work with in the West Midlands?

Service businesses, trades, professional firms, small to mid-size retailers, and tech companies. Recent UK clients include a construction and interiors firm and a pet welfare platform.

I do not take adult, gambling, or categories I would not personally use.

What does Innovator Founder endorsement mean?

Innovator International is one of the UK Home Office's approved endorsing bodies for the Innovator Founder route. My venture SERPGidi, launching in the first quarter of 2027, was assessed by an independent panel as innovative, viable and scalable.

It is a signal of credibility, not a marketing badge. I mention it because it is verifiable.
