02 Pricing

How much should I expect to pay for SEO?

For a West Midlands business, expect £1,500 to £5,000 a month for an ongoing SEO retainer, or £2,500 to £8,000 for a one-off technical audit.

Four things move the price:

  • Site size. A 20-page service site is a different job from a 5,000-product shop.
  • Competition. Ranking for "solicitor Birmingham" takes more than ranking in Walsall for a niche trade.
  • Who does the work. Strategy only costs less than strategy plus content, links and fixes.
  • Reach. One town, the whole region, or the UK.

No surprises. The quote is free, it comes in writing before anything starts, and it is priced on scope, not hours. Retainers run for a three-month minimum.
