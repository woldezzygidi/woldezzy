How to Grow on Social Media: Strategies and Real-World Examples

By 'Wole Oduwole

Nowadays, social media serves a much broader purpose than merely connecting with pals and posting selfies. It's a potent resource for people and companies seeking to build their brand, connect with customers, and build a presence. But how can you increase your visibility and follower count on social media platforms that have millions of users and an endless supply of content? A full-fledged manual with tried-and-true methods and examples from the actual world is available here.

1. Know Your Audience

Understanding Demographics

Before you start posting, it's essential to know who you're talking to. For instance, a skincare brand might target young adults interested in beauty and wellness. Platforms like Instagram and TikTok, popular among this demographic, would be ideal for them.

Tailoring Content

Once you know your audience, tailor your content to their interests and preferences. A fitness influencer, for example, could share workout routines, nutrition tips, and motivational quotes to engage their health-conscious followers.

2. Consistency is Key

Regular Posting

Maintaining a regular posting schedule keeps your audience engaged and your brand top-of-mind. Take "National Geographic," known for its daily posts featuring stunning photography and captivating stories.

Content Calendars

Use content calendars to plan and schedule your posts. This ensures a consistent flow of content and helps you cover a variety of topics.

3. Engage With Your Audience

Interaction

Engagement is a two-way street. Respond to comments, participate in conversations, and maybe even run Q&A sessions. A great example is how celebrities like Dwayne “The Rock” Johnson regularly interact with their fans on social media, building a loyal fanbase.

User-Generated Content

Encourage your audience to share their own content. For instance, Starbucks' #RedCupContest encourages customers to share creative photos of their holiday cups.

4. Use Multimedia

Diverse Content Types

Don’t just stick to text; use images, videos, and infographics. Buzzfeed’s Tasty videos are an excellent example of engaging content that gets shared widely.

Live Streaming

Platforms like Facebook Live and Instagram Live allow real-time interaction. Fitness trainers, for example, use live sessions for workouts, significantly boosting engagement.

5. Collaborate and Partner

Influencer Collaborations

Partner with influencers who align with your brand values. A fashion brand collaborating with a popular style influencer can reach a wider audience.

Cross-Promotion

Work with complementary brands or individuals. A travel blogger partnering with a camera company for a giveaway is a win-win for both.

6. Utilize Analytics

Track Performance

Use analytics tools to track the performance of your posts. This data helps in understanding what type of content resonates with your audience.

Adapt and Evolve

Based on analytics, tweak your strategy. If how-to videos are getting more traction than image posts, it’s a sign to produce more video content.

7. SEO and Hashtags

Optimize Profiles

Use relevant keywords in your profile and posts. This helps in appearing in searches.

Hashtags

Use trending and relevant hashtags to increase the visibility of your posts. A beauty brand might use hashtags like #MakeupTutorial or #SkincareRoutine.

8. Paid Advertising

Targeted Ads

Platforms like Facebook and Instagram offer targeted advertising options to reach specific demographics, helping in gaining new followers.

Sponsored Content

Sponsored posts can boost visibility. Ensure they are clearly labeled and align with your regular content.

Real-World Success Stories

  1. Dove’s Real Beauty Campaign: By promoting body positivity and using real people instead of models, Dove struck a chord with its audience, leading to significant growth on social media.
  2. Airbnb’s User-Generated Content: Airbnb’s strategy to share stories and photos from its users' travels has not only engaged its existing audience but also attracted new followers.
  3. GoPro’s Adventure-Driven Content: GoPro’s use of user-generated adventure and sports content has helped them create a niche community on social media.

Growing on social media is an ongoing process that requires understanding your audience, creating engaging content, and using the right strategies to connect with people. By learning from successful examples and continuously adapting your approach, you can build a strong, engaged social media presence.
