About A short introduction

Twelve years, one rule.

Nigerian-born, based between Lagos and London. Search, growth and product marketing for tech companies that need to compound.

My full name is Adewole Oduwole. Most people call me 'Wole. Online I am Woldezzy. I was born in Nigeria and I have spent the last twelve years working in marketing across three overlapping disciplines: search, growth and product.

I started in the trenches of technical SEO. I then worked on Google product launches across Africa, including the YouTube Music launch in Nigeria and the localisation of Google Maps for the region. After that I spent several years as a growth practitioner and consultant to fintech, edtech and media companies. Along the way I founded SEOGidi, an AI-native SEO platform for startups, where I currently serve as CMO.

SEOGidi is endorsed by Innovator International under the UK Innovator Founder route, which means an independent panel assessed the business as innovative, viable and scalable. I mention it because it is verifiable, not because it is a badge. I am now based in the West Midlands and reading for an MRes at the University of Wolverhampton.

How I work

Everything I do is oriented around one idea: work should compound. If a campaign or a piece of content or an audit does not build on what came before, it is not worth the effort. My best client engagements have been the ones where I could leave the team with systems they could run themselves, not the ones where they needed me forever.

In practice this means being wary of one-off wins, obsessive about baseline measurement, and comfortable saying no to work that will not last. It also means being direct in feedback and slower than average in delivery, because things worth building take time.

What I will not do

Black-hat SEO. Bought links. Fake reviews. Sock puppets. Offshore content farms. Manufactured virality. I have turned down work over each of these. Life is long and the internet is small.

Outside the work

Speaking at events, mostly on SEO and go-to-market in emerging markets. Writing in the journal when something is worth putting down, and occasional longer-form research. Coaching founders when I have bandwidth. Currently reading for an MRes at the University of Wolverhampton.
