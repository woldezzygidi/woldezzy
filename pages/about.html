About A short introduction

Twelve years, one rule.

Nigerian-born, based in the West Midlands, England. SEO and GEO strategist. Search, growth and product marketing for companies that need to compound.

My full name is Adewole Oduwole. Most people call me 'Wole. Online I am Woldezzy. I was born in Nigeria and I have spent the last twelve years working in marketing across three overlapping disciplines: search, growth and product.

I started in the trenches of technical SEO, and the first site I scaled was my own. At TalkGlitz Media, an entertainment news site I founded in 2015, I took monthly organic traffic from 500,000 to over 2 million users by rebuilding the architecture, fixing crawl and indexation, and putting SEO inside the editorial workflow. By late 2020 it was among Nigeria's 50 most visited entertainment news sites. At the same time I ran news SEO for African Examiner, a US-based publication, and grew its organic traffic by 85%.

From 2018 I was at EXPOZE, a Lagos digital agency, first as SEO manager and then as technical product manager, writing the specifications engineers built from. The agency's clients included Google, Meta and Spotify, and I worked on campaigns for the Google Maps anniversary, Spotify Wrapped and Meta product launches in Nigeria.

In 2022 I founded SEOGidi, an SEO platform and agency for startups, where I am CMO. I pitched and won the first 36 clients myself; 60% of them recorded more than 200% growth in reach and conversion in year one, and more than ten went on to raise investment. SEOGidi now serves over 500 clients across the UK and EMEA. Since 2024 I have led its GEO and AEO practice, getting brands cited inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, with a measurement framework I built because none existed.

My next venture, SERPGidi, is endorsed by Innovator International under the UK Innovator Founder route, which means an independent panel assessed the business as innovative, viable and scalable. It launches in the first quarter of 2027. I mention the endorsement because it is verifiable, not because it is a badge. I am now based in the West Midlands, England, where I completed an MRes in International Relations at the University of Wolverhampton. My dissertation, Influencing Without Borders, looked at how Big Tech challenges the global governance framework.

How I work

Everything I do is oriented around one idea: work should compound. If a campaign or a piece of content or an audit does not build on what came before, it is not worth the effort. My best client engagements have been the ones where I could leave the team with systems they could run themselves, not the ones where they needed me forever.

In practice this means being wary of one-off wins, obsessive about baseline measurement, and comfortable saying no to work that will not last. It also means being direct in feedback and slower than average in delivery, because things worth building take time.

Recognition and training

TechPreneur of the Year at the MAYA Awards Africa in 2018. Certified in technical SEO and AI search (Semrush), international SEO (Moz), SEO and search factors (Ahrefs) and advanced Google Analytics (Google), with a diploma in project management. I write about search on Medium and the SEOGidi blog, most recently on AI crawling, pay-to-crawl and multilingual search.

What I will not do

Black-hat SEO. Bought links. Fake reviews. Sock puppets. Offshore content farms. Manufactured virality. I have turned down work over each of these. Life is long and the internet is small.

Outside the work

Speaking at events, mostly on SEO and go-to-market in emerging markets. Writing in the journal when something is worth putting down, and occasional longer-form research. Coaching founders when I have bandwidth.
