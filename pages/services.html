How I take on work

Every engagement starts with a short call. I do not use questionnaires or intake forms. The goal is to understand whether the work is worth doing and whether I am the right person to do it. If the answer to either is no, I will say so.

I take on a small number of clients at a time. One-off audits are possible but usually a false economy for anything above a small site.

Rates are quoted in scope, not hours. I do not do daily rates for strategic work.
